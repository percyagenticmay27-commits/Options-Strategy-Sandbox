# Barney Strategy Matrix — controlled paper experiments

**Effective:** November 18, 2026. **Scope:** OPTION-001 and the independent Options Strategy Sandbox. **Execution authority:** local paper only; no broker orders. Research alternatives are **not** automatic live or paper replacements until separately tested and approved.

## Why these experiments are separated

1. **Entry experiment A/B:** Existing `EARLY_REVERSAL_EMA9_20_SHADOW` is the control. The test has precisely one new variable: completed-bar **EMA 50 price-and-slope confirmation**. Bull put bias requires price above VWAP and EMA50, slope positive; bearish call bias requires price below both, slope negative. Log the control decision and test decision on the **same timestamp and provider evidence**. The test never edits the control ledger. Historical November 18 10:04 ET bullish signal had a negative EMA50 slope and is **test-rejected**.
2. **Defense experiment A/B:** Existing close/hold and opposite-side credit adjustment is the control. On the same observation, independently record **close-now executable gross P/L**, original max risk, the proposed adjustment's incremental credit, new maximum loss, and remaining risk. No fabricated prices, projected probability, or expected value. Keep a separate comparison ledger when complete data arrives; observe the effects before approving a new defense policy. Missing independent price/volume evidence means the alternative is `DATA_UNAVAILABLE`, never a simulated fill.
3. **Menu research:** BWB and debit structures are additional **candidate choices** only. One new family at a time; require matching verified option IDs, original quote times, same-day/valid expiry, complete legs, fresh bid/ask/size, full maximum loss including costs, a newer second quote batch for a proposed fill, and existing schedule/event/trend rules. Preserve original hard stop and mandatory exits. No roll/repair that increases unapproved account risk.

## Close-versus-defend decision record

For each proposed defense record: timestamp; original symbol/legs/credit; original current natural buyback and realized-if-closed gross P/L; original maximum loss and stop; actual incremental premium of proposed opposite-side legs; final max risk and worst loss **on either side**; full four-leg buyback mark; distance from new short to verified resistance/support; remaining minutes to expiry; current gamma, spreads and sizes; fees/slippage assumptions; available daily loss budget; and reason for HOLD, CLOSE, DEFEND, or DATA_UNAVAILABLE. A reduced maximum loss is **not by itself sufficient** to claim improved expectancy. Do not infer a win probability from delta alone.

Keep `barney_comparison` fields on OPTION-001 held positions and a distinct research output in Sandbox. The comparison is observational and cannot override an existing required exit. Market data that would make a new trade valid is not permission to change an already-held position's hard stop.

## Scoring and experiment governance

Evaluate identical forward sessions, with the control and the test evaluated against the **same timestamped bars and option data**. Measure sample size, acceptance/rejection reasons, missed winning trades, closed-only win rate, realized P/L, net expectancy, fees, maximum intraday drawdown when fully observed, maximum loss taken, adjustment count and incremental P/L attributable to an adjustment. Flag unclosed/pending settlement separately. Review at least 30 matched sessions as an initial audit; do not promote solely on 30 observations. Freeze each rule set and get explicit promotion approval. No autonomous escalation from research-only to execution.

## Repository-specific implementation

- **Trade-Option-Bot:** `src/option_bot/barney_strategy_matrix.py` is an observation-only pure comparator and EMA50 A/B classifier. The Early Reversal report includes `ema50_experiment`. The local paper manager stores `barney_comparison` and retains existing exits and defensive controls. `scripts/barney_alpaca.py` remains read-only quote management; it cannot directly execute alternatives.
- **Options-Strategy-Sandbox:** Existing `bwb.py` performs BWB research and `sniper.py` performs debit-spread research. `barney_matrix.py` mirrors the strategy research menu and consumes already-verified candidate results; it neither produces fills nor writes to the Option Bot ledger. Use this as a forward experiment scorecard, not a trading authority.

**Paper only:** `broker_submissions=0`; no broker transport for any new matrix option. Never fabricate option contracts, volume-profile levels, EMA75, theoretical POP, or second-batch fills.
