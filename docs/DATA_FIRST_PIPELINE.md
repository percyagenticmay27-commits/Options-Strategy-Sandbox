## Freshness rules

Executable option quotes, stock/index reference quotes and publication envelopes retain <=60-second limits. A completed five-minute candle is validated against the expected latest completed five-minute boundary in the current regular session. A finite 60-second publication grace allows the immediately preceding candle just after a boundary; older, future, off-grid or prior-session signal candles fail. Candle close timestamps are never restamped to appear current. Historical warmup bars retain their true old timestamps.

## Outputs and limits

The preflight produces a collection plan, not a buy or a promise of profitability. Strategy engines still compute natural-price economics, metadata, calendar checks, defined loss and second-quote confirmation. Existing allocations, risks, entry/exit windows, bullish credit-profile ownership, and all ledgers are preserved. BWB's bar-volume profile remains a labeled approximation rather than tick-level volume-at-price.
